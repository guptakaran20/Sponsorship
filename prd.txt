
# 📄 PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

## 🧩 Product Name (Working Title)

**SponsorBridge**
*Tagline: Connecting Campus Innovation with Industry Support*

---

## 1. 📌 Problem Statement

College clubs and student organizations regularly organize events, fests, hackathons, and workshops that require financial or in-kind sponsorship.

However:

* Clubs struggle to **find relevant sponsors**
* They rely heavily on:

  * Cold emailing
  * Personal contacts
  * Alumni network
  * Random LinkedIn DMs
* Companies receive **unstructured sponsorship requests**
* There is:

  * No centralized platform
  * No credibility verification
  * No ROI tracking for companies

➡️ Result:

* Clubs miss funding opportunities
* Companies miss campus visibility
* Sponsorship ecosystem remains inefficient and trust-deficient

---

## 2. 🎯 Goal

Create a **two-sided marketplace platform** that connects:

🏫 College Clubs ↔ 🏢 Companies

To:

* Discover sponsorship opportunities
* Pitch and apply for sponsorships
* Track deliverables
* Build long-term partnerships

---

## 3. 👥 Target Users

### Primary Users

| User Type               | Description                                          |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Club Admin              | Fest organizers, technical clubs, cultural societies |
| Company Marketing Teams | Brand managers, CSR teams, hiring teams              |
| Startup Founders        | Early stage companies wanting visibility             |

---

### Secondary Users

* Event Attendees (future feature)
* Alumni Sponsors
* Campus Influencers
* Vendor Partners

---

## 4. 🛠️ User Roles

### A. College Club

* Register with:

  * College Email Verification
  * Club Description
  * Past Events
  * Audience Reach
  * Social Media Stats
* Create Event Listings
* Upload Sponsorship Deck
* Define Sponsorship Tiers:

  * Title Sponsor
  * Gold
  * Silver
  * Merchandise Partner
* Track sponsor applications

---

### B. Company

* Register Business Profile
* Select Sponsorship Interests:

  * Tech Events
  * Cultural Fests
  * Sports Events
  * Hackathons
* Set:

  * Budget Range
  * Preferred Cities
  * Audience Type
* Browse Event Listings
* Sponsor directly or request proposal

---
